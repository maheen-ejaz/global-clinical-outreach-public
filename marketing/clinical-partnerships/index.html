Clinical listings will extend these services and help prospective applicants understand programme requirements; each provider confirms eligibility and makes admission decisions.

Questions

Before we talk

Is the new clinical collection live?

Not yet. Existing GooCampusWorld programmes are live; the new clinical collection will launch once the initial provider group and programme details are agreed.

Which programmes are relevant?

Observerships, electives, clinical attachments and fellowships.

Who are these programmes for?

Medical students and graduates. International eligibility depends on each provider’s requirements.

Who handles enquiries and applications?

We’ll agree with each provider who handles enquiries, applications and applicant support.

What are the commercial terms?

We’ll discuss terms privately with each provider.

What should I send?

A short programme overview, a webpage if available, and the relevant contact. No student records or detailed submission are needed.

What if I’m not the right person?

Please point us to the colleague responsible for the programme or institutional partnerships.
